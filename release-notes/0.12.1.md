## Fixes

- **Milestone-plan no longer wipes its own plan.** After the planning meeting committed the milestones, the P21 diagram meeting (which draws those milestones as structural diagrams) ran in the same workflow — and when an agent re-stated the milestone plan while drawing it, the stage-leak filter treated that re-statement as a disallowed decision and **deleted the milestone files the plan had just committed**. Silently — the deletion bypassed the milestone registry, so it didn't even leave an unlink-log entry; the plan simply went empty. The decision-filter now never deletes milestone files: milestones are owned solely by the milestone snapshot (logged + guarded) and explicit retraction, and a re-emitted plan is treated as a re-affirmation, not a stage-leak. (Latent in any workflow that disallows `milestone_plan`, e.g. tdd-design — the diagram meeting just made it bite every run.)
