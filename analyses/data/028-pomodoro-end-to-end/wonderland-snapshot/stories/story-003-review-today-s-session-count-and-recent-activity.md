## Story 003: Review today's session count and recent activity

**Persona:** James, 45, therapist who uses Pomodoro for admin work (note-writing, email, billing). Wants to see at a glance how many sessions he's completed today and whether he's on track for his personal goal of 8 sessions.

**Situation:**

It's 3 PM and James has completed some sessions this morning. He opens the app to remember how many are logged and whether he has time for another block before his 4 PM client.

**Need:**

As James, I want to see how many sessions I've completed today and what time I started them, so that I can decide whether I have time for another block.

**Acceptance:**
- The home screen or a dedicated 'History' tab shows today's session count (e.g., '4 sessions today')
- Below the count, a list shows each session with start time and duration (e.g., '10:15 AM — 25 min', '11:00 AM — 25 min')
- The total focus time is shown (e.g., '100 minutes today')
- The view updates in real-time as new sessions complete

**Tier:** core

**Confusion-flags:**
- Should breaks be shown in the history, or only sessions? I'm assuming sessions only, but breaks might be useful for people tracking 'total session time' vs. 'total time with app.'
