## Story 003: Review today's session count

**Persona:** Jordan, 26, a student and gig worker who uses pomodoros to track productivity across multiple jobs. Numbers feel like progress.

**Situation:**

It's evening. Jordan wants to know how many focus sessions they completed today. They feel scattered and want evidence that they actually worked.

**Need:**

As Jordan, I want to see how many sessions I completed today at a glance, so that I have a concrete measure of the work I did, even if the day felt chaotic.

**Acceptance:**
- The home screen or a dedicated 'today' view shows today's completed session count
- The count includes only sessions that ran to completion (not abandoned sessions)
- The view also shows total focus time today (e.g., '6 sessions = 150 minutes')
- Jordan can see this without navigating deep into menus

**Tier:** core

**Confusion-flags:**
- What counts as 'completed'? A session that ran the full 25 minutes? A session where the notification fired? A session the user confirmed they finished? This affects what the count means.
- Should today reset at midnight, or at some other time? That's a settings question, but the default assumption matters.
