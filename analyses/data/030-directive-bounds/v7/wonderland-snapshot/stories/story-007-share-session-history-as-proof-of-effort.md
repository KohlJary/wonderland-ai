## Story 007: Share session history as proof of effort

**Persona:** Kenji, 31, a freelancer who sometimes needs to show clients evidence of focused work. Not billable hours, but proof that he scheduled deep work.

**Situation:**

Kenji finishes a project. A client asks, 'How much time did you spend on this?' He wants to show a weekly total or a specific-day breakdown without exporting spreadsheets.

**Need:**

As Kenji, I want to export or share my session history for a given week or day, so I can answer questions about my work effort without manually tracking.

**Acceptance:**
- Kenji can select a date range in the history view and generate a simple report (text or image)
- The report shows total sessions and total hours for that range
- Kenji can copy the report or share it directly (via clipboard, email, or simple link) without installing new tools

**Tier:** fast-follow

**Confusion-flags:**
- This assumes freelance use cases matter. Do they? The M1 directive says 'single-user local app,' which suggests personal use, not professional accounting. This story might be premature.
- What does 'share' mean technically? Email? Screenshot? Export to PDF? I don't know the platform yet, and this ambiguity matters.
