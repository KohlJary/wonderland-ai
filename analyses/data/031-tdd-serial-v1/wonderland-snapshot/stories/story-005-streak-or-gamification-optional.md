## Story 005: Streak or Gamification (Optional)

**Persona:** Kenji, 19, college student, easily distracted, responds well to visible progress markers. A simple 'sessions this week' counter makes the abstract concept of 'consistency' concrete.

**Situation:**

Kenji uses Pomodoros inconsistently — some weeks he runs five a day, other weeks he forgets the app exists. A visible weekly count would give him a reason to actually show up.

**Need:**

As Kenji, I want to see how many focus sessions I've completed this week, so that I can feel the momentum of consistency and stay motivated.

**Acceptance:**
- Weekly session count is displayed prominently (or on the daily review)
- Count resets every Monday (or configurable day)
- No judgment if count is low — it's a mirror, not a report card

**Tier:** fast-follow

**Confusion-flags:**
- Unclear: is this motivating or infantilizing? (Depends entirely on tone/design — need to see the actual UI to know if it lands right)
- Unclear: does Kenji see a monthly view too, or just weekly? What's the right time horizon to feel momentum without obsessing?
