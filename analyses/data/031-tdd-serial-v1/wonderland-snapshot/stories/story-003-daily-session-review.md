## Story 003: Daily Session Review

**Persona:** David, 52, consultant, uses Pomodoros to structure his day. At 5pm he wants a snapshot: 'How many focus sessions did I complete today? Did I stick to my breaks?' He uses this to reflect on focus quality, not to shame himself.

**Situation:**

David finishes his workday. He wants to see a simple summary of his Pomodoro performance so he can assess whether his focus structure actually worked, and adjust tomorrow if needed.

**Need:**

As David, I want to see a daily summary of completed focus sessions, skipped breaks, and total focus time, so that I can reflect on whether my time structure served me.

**Acceptance:**
- Daily review shows count of completed focus sessions
- Daily review shows count of completed vs. skipped breaks
- Daily review shows total focus time (sum of all completed sessions)
- Daily review is available at any time, not just end-of-day
- Data rolls over to a new day at midnight

**Tier:** core

**Confusion-flags:**
- Unclear: does David see a calendar/history of past days, or just today? (affects whether this is reflection or just 'today's score')
- Unclear: what counts as a 'completed' session — is it touching start and reaching the end, or does pausing mid-session mean it doesn't count?
