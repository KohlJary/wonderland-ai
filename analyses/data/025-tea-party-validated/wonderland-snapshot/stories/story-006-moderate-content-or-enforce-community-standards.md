## Story 006: Moderate content or enforce community standards

**Persona:** This is framed as a story but I'm genuinely uncertain whether it belongs in the MVP. Let me name the persona anyway: Robin, a platform moderator or the person running this server. They need to handle abuse.

**Situation:**

Someone has created a page with hateful content, spam, or material that violates the law in the EU. Robin needs to remove it.

**Need:**

As a platform operator, I need tools to view reported content, suspend accounts, and delete pages that violate our terms, so that the platform doesn't become a vector for abuse.

**Acceptance:**
- There is a report mechanism (or admin panel)
- Reported pages can be reviewed and deleted
- Accounts can be suspended or deleted for violation

**Tier:** fast-follow

**Confusion-flags:**
- This is the responsibility of the platform operator, not the end user. It might not belong in MVP at all — can we launch with a 'contact me if there's illegal content' email? Or does EU law require moderation tooling from day one?
- If we build moderation, it's a Queen of Hearts decision (legal liability), not something I should be scoping. I'm flagging it but it may belong in a separate directive about compliance.
