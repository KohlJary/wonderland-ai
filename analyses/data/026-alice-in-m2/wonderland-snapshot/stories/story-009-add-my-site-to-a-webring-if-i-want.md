## Story 009: Add my site to a webring if I want

**Persona:** Dex, 27, nostalgic for old internet culture. Knows what webrings are and wants to join one to be part of a community. Wants the ability to link to 'previous' and 'next' sites in a curated list.

**Situation:**

Dex created a homepage about vintage computing. They want to join a webring with other people who care about the same topic, and they want their page to have 'previous/next' buttons linking to neighbors.

**Need:**

As Dex, I want to opt into a webring and have my page linked with other members' pages, so that visitors can discover related homepages through a curated network.

**Acceptance:**
- A webring management page lists available webrings (curated, not user-created)
- Dex can opt in or out of a webring
- When opted in, Dex's page displays prev/next links pointing to neighbors
- Webring membership is visible on the profile/discovery page

**Tier:** fast-follow

**Confusion-flags:**
- Webring curation: who decides which webrings exist? Is this moderated? For MVP, is this even a priority, or is a simple 'recent activity' discovery better? The directive lists it as an option ('a webring, your call'). This feels like a Rabbit/Cat decision: feasibility and priority.
