## Story 008: See the most recently active homepages (alternative to directory)

**Persona:** Casey, 31, likes serendipity but also likes currency. Wants to find homepages that were updated *today* or *this week*, not just any recent page.

**Situation:**

Casey visits the Discover page and sees a 'recently updated' section showing pages that changed in the past week. Casey wants to keep coming back to find fresh content.

**Need:**

As Casey, I want to see pages sorted by 'last updated' so that I can find active people and fresh content, and come back to the platform regularly to check for updates.

**Acceptance:**
- Discover page has a 'recently updated' view, sortable by last-update timestamp
- 'Recently updated' is the default or prominent sort option
- Updates are timestamped accurately (if someone edits their page, the timestamp updates)

**Tier:** enrichment

**Confusion-flags:**
- The directive mentions 'webring' as an alternative discovery mechanism. Are both needed for MVP? A webring is curation + community; recent activity is algorithmic currency. They serve different impulses. This feels like a Rabbit scope question, but the user experience feels materially different.
