## Story 007: Share my homepage link and know people can visit

**Persona:** Riley, 29, a graphic designer. Built a homepage and wants to share it on Twitter. They want to know: does the link work? Do people see my page when they click?

**Situation:**

Riley copies their homepage URL (/~riley) and posts it on social media. An hour later, a friend clicks it and... Riley has no idea whether it worked.

**Need:**

As Riley, I want to share my homepage URL and be confident that visitors can see my page, so that I can promote my work without worrying that something is broken.

**Acceptance:**
- Visiting /~[username] from outside, logged-out, shows the public page
- Page loads without requiring login
- Page displays correctly in all common browsers (desktop and mobile)
- Links to other users' pages work (Priya can click 'discover' and land on public pages)

**Tier:** core

**Confusion-flags:**
- The assumption that public homepages are truly accessible without login feels worth verifying — it's the core promise of a shareable platform.
