## Story 003: Review today's session count

**Persona:** Devon, 41, a consultant who tracks his own productivity as a proxy for focus quality. He wants a quick number at the end of the day: how many deep-work sessions did I actually complete?

**Situation:**

It's 6 PM. Devon is wrapping up his workday. He opens the pomodoro app and wants to know: did I get five sessions in today, or four? The number matters to him as a progress signal.

**Need:**

As Devon, I want to see a count of completed sessions today, so that I can assess my own focus and productivity at a glance.

**Acceptance:**
- The app shows a 'Today' view that displays the number of completed sessions
- The count is always visible on the main screen or accessible with one tap
- The count resets daily at midnight (or at a user-configurable time)
- The count increments only when a session completes; abandoned sessions don't count

**Tier:** core

**Confusion-flags:**
- Does 'today' mean calendar day or the last 24 hours? For a shift worker or someone in a different timezone, this matters.
- Should the app show partial/abandoned sessions separately, or only completed ones? The user probably cares about completion rate, not volume.
