## Story 003: Review today's session count

**Persona:** David, 42, a project manager who uses the tracker to understand his own focus patterns and to set a daily target (usually 6–8 sessions per day).

**Situation:**

It's 4 PM and David wants to know whether he's hit his target for the day. He opens the app and wants to see at a glance how many sessions he's completed.

**Need:**

As David, I want to see today's session count and a visual indicator of progress toward my typical target, so that I can decide whether to keep working or call it a day.

**Acceptance:**
- The home screen or a 'today' view shows today's session count as a prominent number
- Sessions are counted only if they were completed (not abandoned, not skipped)
- The count updates immediately after each session ends
- A secondary view shows the total break time taken today

**Tier:** core

**Confusion-flags:**
- What is 'today'? Midnight-to-midnight? Or does it reset at noon for shift workers? This feels like it matters for David's mental model of his day.
