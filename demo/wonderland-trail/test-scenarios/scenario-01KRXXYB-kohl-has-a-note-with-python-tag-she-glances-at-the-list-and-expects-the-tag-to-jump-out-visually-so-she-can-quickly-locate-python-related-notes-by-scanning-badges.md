## Scenario 160: Kohl has a note with 'python' tag; she glances at the list and expects the tag to jump out visually so she can quickly locate python-related notes by scanning badges

**GUID:** 01KRXXYBA04FZFE3AR0DTZ7BWK
**Severity:** degradation

**Setup:**

Kohl has 50 notes across her research. About 15 of them are tagged 'python'. She wants to quickly find the python notes without typing into the search box. She opens the note list and scans for the 'python' badges.

**Trigger:**

Kohl's eyes scan down the note list, looking for the 'python' badge visually (color, shape, position).

**Expected:**

The 'python' badge (and all tag badges) are visually consistent in color, shape, and position across all notes. The badges are small enough not to obscure the note title, but large enough and distinct enough in color/shape that they stand out when scanning (e.g., not a light gray color that blends into the background).

**Concern:**

If badges are poorly contrasted or positioned inconsistently, Kohl has to slow down and read tag names rather than scan them. This degrades her discovery experience from 'glance and locate' to 'read and find', which wastes cognitive load.

**Property:**

Tag badges have sufficient visual contrast and consistent styling to support quick visual scanning

**Implies:**
- Badge background color has sufficient contrast against the page background (WCAG AA or better)
- Badge styling is consistent across all notes (same color, shape, size for all tags)
- Badge positioning is consistent (always below title, always left-aligned, or always in same row position)
- Font size and weight is readable at a glance (not too small, not too large)
