## Story 016: Kohl finds a past note by title or content

**GUID:** 01KRXRMEHCCPN14TM6J8PGJD7T

**Persona:** Kohl, 30, AI Researcher. After a productive session, he has 20+ notes accumulated. A week later, a reviewer asks about a specific experiment; Kohl needs to locate the right note quickly.

**Situation:**

Kohl opens his app and sees his note list. He can scan by title, but some titles are vague ('expt-2024-01-15'). He needs a way to search or filter notes to find the one about, say, attention-head variance.

**Need:**

As Kohl, I want to search or filter my notes by title or content snippet, so that I can find a specific past observation without scrolling through my entire archive.

**Acceptance:**
- Kohl sees a list of all his notes with titles visible
- Kohl can type a search term and the list filters to show only notes whose title or body contains that term
- Search is case-insensitive and substring-matching (searching 'attention' finds 'attention-head variance')
- Search results appear instantly as Kohl types (no 'search' button required)

**Tier:** core

**Confusion-flags:**
- The 14 foundation stories don't include a note listing or search UI — this is a user-facing flow that the foundation enables but doesn't yet specify. The foundation stories are all about the backend schema + frontend editor; this story names what Kohl does after he's written a note.
- Tags are mentioned in the foundation stories but don't appear in Kohl's primary flow here. Search by content is his observed need; tagging might be enrichment.
